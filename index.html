Courses

Persuasive writing in grant writing (US focus)

6 modules · flexible delivery Click for details

A highly interactive, workshop-style course on NIH grant applications, aimed at US-based researchers. It is organised in six modules and can be delivered flexibly, either as a three-day intensive or spread over several weeks. Exercises take place in small breakout groups, so participants have more time to discuss their work and get to know colleagues from other parts of the institution. The facilitators then lead a plenary discussion with the whole group. A 30-minute one-to-one session with personalised feedback on the participant's own abstract can be arranged on request.

Persuasive writing in grant writing (EU focus)

6 modules · flexible delivery Click for details

A highly interactive, workshop-style course on grant applications for researchers based in the UK and Europe. The examples and exercises draw on ERC, Horizon Europe (including MSCA fellowships), UKRI, Wellcome and NIHR applications. The course is organised in six modules and can be delivered flexibly, either as a three-day intensive or spread over several weeks. Exercises take place in small breakout groups, so participants have more time to discuss their work and get to know colleagues from other parts of the institution. The facilitators then lead a plenary discussion with the whole group. A 30-minute one-to-one session with personalised feedback on the participant's own abstract can be arranged on request.

Mission

PubliMentor exists to advance science by making academic publishing clearer, fairer, and more robust. We bring hands‑on editorial experience and deep knowledge of COPE guidance to help communities publish trustworthy research at pace — without compromising integrity.

  • Practical, real‑world solutions
  • Ethics‑first, author‑positive approach
  • Capacity building for lasting impact
